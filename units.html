EXTRAExtra Credit: Personal Financial Plan

There is one extra credit assignment. It becomes available only to students who have passed all nineteen unit quizzes, and it must be submitted complete, as partial submissions are not accepted. It is due Friday, December 18. Passing one quiz per due date is enough to get through the course, but it will not leave you time to finish the extra credit. If you want it, work ahead.

What You Build

  • A financial plan from your current age to age 85, real or imaginary
  • One spreadsheet file, clearly labeled, tabs allowed
  • A written report, 2–3 pages, double-spaced

What Is Graded

  • Presentation: easy to read and follow, with points for creativity
  • Accuracy: correct calculations, believable and realistic numbers
  • Writing: explanation and justification of your choices

Required Spreadsheet Components

Income and Taxes

  • Annual income before taxes
  • Estimated annual taxes, including income
  • Monthly and annual income after taxes

Monthly Expenses

  • Housing (rent or mortgage payments)
  • Auto / public transportation
  • Three other expenses (food, leisure, home maintenance, etc.)

Banking and Investing

  • Checking account with no interest rate
  • Ordinary savings account with an estimated interest rate
  • Retirement account (IRA, tax-deferred IRA)
  • Mutual funds and stocks

Debt

  • Student loan debt
  • Credit card debt
  • Mortgage (30 year, fixed rate)

Research Sources

Cite any researched values in your report. These are good places to start:

  • Wages by profession, state, and region: bls.gov/oes/tables.htm
  • Home price indices by region: fhfa.gov/data/hpi/datasets
  • Rent estimates and housing data: data.hud.gov/data_sets.html
  • Federal income tax rates: irs.gov › tax rates and brackets
  • 30-year mortgage rates: fred.stlouisfed.org/series/MORTGAGE30US
